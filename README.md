## About

Clear Night Coach tells you the one best thing to point your scope at tonight — matched to your rig, your sky, and tonight's conditions — and explains why. The nightly decision works fully offline.

This repository hosts release downloads only. Support and documentation live at [clearnightcoach.com](https://clearnightcoach.com).
